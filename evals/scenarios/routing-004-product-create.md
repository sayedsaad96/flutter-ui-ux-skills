# Scenario: New cross-platform Flutter product

## User request

"Create the UI/UX foundation for a new Flutter learning app for children, targeting Android, iOS, tablets, and web. It should feel playful but not chaotic, support Arabic and English, and use animation to make progress and feedback feel alive."

## Available evidence

- Product requirements only.
- No existing code, screenshots, Figma, or runtime.

## Required behavior

- Mode: CREATE.
- Complexity: PRODUCT.
- Do not jump directly into widgets.
- Establish product/audience context and design direction first.
- Recognize the relevance of design-system, responsive/adaptive, inclusive, motion, engineering, polish, and later verification capabilities.
- Do not invent evidence or claim runtime validation.
